Map textures for Step Forward
- gevel_*, baksteen*, beton*, golf_*, dak_*, weg*, asfalt*, stoep*, grind, kinderkop (v106):
  CC0 (public domain) photo-scanned textures from polyhaven.com (via the Papyszoo/CC0-Public-Domain-Textures mirror on GitHub),
  downscaled to 512px.
- muur_*, plafond, plank, dambord*, lambris, parket, tegels, pleister1-4, behang1 (v107): CC0 photo-scanned textures from polyhaven.com
  (same mirror), downscaled to 512px. zak (sandbags): polyhaven hessian_380, CC0.
- deur_*, raam_*:
  cut from texture sheets generated by the game's author (Kling AI), made seamless with a script.
- asfalt, asfalt2, pleister1-4, behang1-2, parket, tegels, stoep2:
  CC0 (public domain) textures from ambientCG.com and polyhaven.com.
- art/meubel/: furniture props cut from sheets generated by the game's author (Kling AI).
